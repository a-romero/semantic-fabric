# Cash ISA

A Cash ISA is a type of ISA that holds cash savings and pays tax-free interest.
Aviva's Cash ISA has an annual subscription limit set by HMRC. A Cash ISA is a
lower-risk product than a Stocks and Shares ISA because the balance is not exposed
to market movements.
